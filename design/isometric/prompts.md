## Alternatif hangat diorama

Use case: stylized-concept. Asset type: isometric foodcourt scene artwork for a mobile calorie tracking app, no UI text. Primary request: polished Indonesian neighborhood foodcourt diorama, warm and friendly. Ten compact kiosks around one central dining table, categories visually recognizable through food props: warteg rice dishes, mie and bakso, gorengan, drinks, soto, sate, nasi padang, pecel, bubur, grilled fish. One tiny friendly courier carrying a plate from a kiosk toward the central table. Style/medium: premium 3D clay and painted wood miniature, clear isometric camera at 30 degrees, rich tactile materials and soft ambient occlusion. Composition: square landscape, all scene elements fully within frame, central table clear, distinct readable silhouettes, no phones or app chrome, no written words, no lettering, no numbers, no watermark. Palette: terracotta, turmeric yellow, sage green, warm cream. Keep the table and route legible at small mobile size.
